我们深知个人信息对您的重要性，并会尽全力保护您的隐私安全。请您在使用我们的产品或服务前，仔细阅读并了解本隐私政策。

一、我们是谁

赛博拼豆（CyberBeadArt）是一款拼豆艺术创作工具，由开发者彭龙鑫（以下简称"我们"）运营。

联系邮箱：splongxl@gmail.com

二、我们收集的信息

我们仅收集为实现产品功能所必需的最少信息：

2.1 您主动提供的信息

  • Apple ID 登录信息：当您使用"通过 Apple 登录"时，我们获取 Apple 提供的用户标识符、显示名称和邮箱地址（如您选择共享）。
  • 昵称：您可以设置在社区中显示的昵称。
  • 用户创作内容：您上传到社区的拼豆作品图片和描述。

2.2 我们自动收集的信息

  • 照片/相机：仅在您主动选择照片或拍照时访问，用于创建拼豆图纸。我们不会在后台访问您的相册。
  • 本地存储：项目数据（拼豆图纸、进度）存储在您设备本地。
  • 应用偏好设置：主题、语言等设置保存在设备本地（UserDefaults）。

2.3 我们不收集的信息

  • 我们不收集设备标识符（IDFA/IDFV）
  • 我们不收集位置信息
  • 我们不收集通讯录、短信、通话记录
  • 我们不使用任何第三方分析或广告 SDK
  • 我们不跟踪您在其他 App 中的活动

三、我们如何使用信息

  • 提供拼豆创作核心功能（图片像素化、珠子放置、烫熨、导出）
  • 实现用户登录和身份认证
  • 在社区中显示您的作品和昵称
  • 保存您的创作进度和应用设置

四、信息存储与安全

您的项目数据存储在设备本地文件系统中。

登录凭证通过 iOS Keychain 安全存储，采用系统级加密保护。

社区相关数据通过腾讯云 CloudBase 服务存储和传输，采用 HTTPS 加密通信。

五、信息共享

我们不会将您的个人信息出售、出租或交易给任何第三方。

以下情况除外：

  • 您上传到社区的作品将对其他用户可见（这是社区功能的本质）
  • 法律法规要求或政府主管部门依法要求时
  • 为保护用户安全和公共利益所必需时

六、第三方服务

  • Apple 登录服务：受 Apple 隐私政策 约束
  • 腾讯云 CloudBase：用于社区数据存储，受 腾讯云隐私政策 约束

七、您的权利

您有权：

  • 访问和查看您的个人信息
  • 修改您的昵称和个人资料
  • 删除您上传的社区作品
  • 注销您的账户（联系我们处理）
  • 撤回对照片/相机权限的授权（通过系统设置）

八、未成年人保护

我们的产品主要面向成年用户。如果您是未满 14 周岁的未成年人，请在监护人的陪同和指导下使用本应用，并请监护人代为阅读本隐私政策。

九、隐私政策更新

我们可能会适时修订本隐私政策。更新后的隐私政策将在本页面发布，重大变更时我们会在 App 内通知您。

Privacy Policy

CyberBeadArt  |  Last Updated: April 20, 2026  |  Effective: April 20, 2026

We understand the importance of your personal information and are committed to protecting your privacy. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using our product or services.

1. Who We Are

CyberBeadArt is a bead art creation tool operated by developer Longxin Peng (hereinafter referred to as "we" or "us").

Contact email: splongxl@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

We only collect the minimum information necessary to provide our services:

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Apple ID Login: When you use "Sign in with Apple", we receive your user identifier, display name, and email address (if you choose to share) provided by Apple.
  • Nickname: You may set a display name for the community.
  • User-Created Content: Bead art images and descriptions you upload to the community.

2.2 Information We Automatically Collect

  • Photos/Camera: Accessed only when you actively select a photo or take one, used to create bead art patterns. We do not access your photo library in the background.
  • Local Storage: Project data (bead patterns, progress) is stored locally on your device.
  • App Preferences: Settings such as theme and language are saved locally on your device (UserDefaults).

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect device identifiers (IDFA/IDFV)
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not collect contacts, messages, or call logs
  • We do not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs
  • We do not track your activity across other apps

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide core bead art creation features (photo pixelation, bead placement, ironing, export)
  • Enable user login and authentication
  • Display your artwork and nickname in the community
  • Save your creation progress and app settings

4. Data Storage and Security

Your project data is stored in the local file system on your device.

Login credentials are securely stored via iOS Keychain with system-level encryption.

Community-related data is stored and transmitted through Tencent CloudBase services using HTTPS encrypted communication.

5. Information Sharing

We will not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

Exceptions include:

  • Artworks you upload to the community will be visible to other users (this is inherent to the community feature)
  • When required by laws, regulations, or government authorities
  • When necessary to protect user safety and public interest

6. Third-Party Services

  • Apple Sign-In: Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy
  • Tencent CloudBase: Used for community data storage, subject to Tencent Cloud Privacy Policy

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and view your personal information
  • Modify your nickname and profile
  • Delete your community uploads
  • Delete your account (contact us to process)
  • Revoke photo/camera permissions (via system Settings)

8. Children's Privacy

Our product is primarily intended for adult users. If you are under 14 years of age, please use this app under the supervision and guidance of a guardian, and ask your guardian to read this Privacy Policy on your behalf.

9. Policy Updates

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be published on this page, and we will notify you within the App for significant changes.
